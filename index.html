Common questions

My shifts look wrong

Check Settings → Your platoon is set correctly. If you changed platoons, pick the new one and choose Yes, I'm moving platoons from today so your past roster stays on your old platoon.

My annual leave dates look wrong

Check Settings → Annual leave group. If you've swapped or deferred leave, log it in Annuals → Leave swaps.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Turn on Settings → Backup & data → Back up to iCloud automatically on your old phone. On the new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID and open the app: it offers to restore your latest iCloud backup. You can also pick an older one with Restore from iCloud.

No iCloud, or a different Apple ID? On the old phone tap Save a backup file and keep it somewhere safe. On the new phone tap Restore from a file and pick it.

Are the pay estimates exact?

No – they're estimates before tax, from the ACT Fire & Rescue Enterprise Agreement 2024–2026 or the rates you enter. Always check your payslip.

I'm not getting reminders

Check your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Firies Friend is allowed, and the switches in the app's Settings → Notifications are on.
