Privacy Policy

Firies Friend · Last updated 19 November 2026

Firies Friend ("the app") is an independent roster, logbook and leave app for firefighters, made by Stephen Holmes. In short: we never receive any of your information. It stays on your phone, unless you choose to back it up to your own iCloud Drive or share it.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We can't see anything you put in the app.

Your information

Everything you enter – shifts, overtime, allowances, changes of shift, leave, notes, phone book contacts, recipes and settings – is stored on your device. It is never sent to us. It only leaves your phone in the ways described below, all of which you choose.

Location

When Suggest allowances at stations is on and you've allowed location access (your iPhone asks first), the app checks your location only while it's open to see if you're at a fire station, so it can suggest logging an allowance or overtime. Your location is compared with station locations on your phone. That check is never stored, sent or shared. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

If you tap I'm here – save this station's location on a station in the phone book, the app saves that spot as the station's pin. The pin stays on your phone and is included in your backups. It is only passed to Maps when you open that station in Maps. Tapping the button again at the station replaces the pin.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (allowances, leave forms and changes of shift) are scheduled on your device by the app. No notification data leaves your phone.

Calendar

If you turn on calendar sync, the app adds your shifts to a "Firies Friend" calendar on your iPhone. iPhone asks you for full calendar access first: the app needs it to find its own calendar and to update or remove the shifts it added. It doesn't read, change or delete your other events.

Like your other calendars, the Firies Friend calendar may sync to your other devices through your calendar account (for example iCloud). That sync is handled by Apple or your calendar provider, not by us. Turning calendar sync off removes the shifts the app added.

Photos and files

If you check a payslip (PDF or screenshot) or scan a recipe photo, it's read on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. The camera and Photos are only used when you choose to take or pick a picture, and saving a roster picture or recipe card to Photos only happens when you tap Save Image.

Handy apps

The icons in Handy apps are built into Firies Friend, so nothing is requested from the internet to show them. Tapping an app opens its page in the App Store.

Tips

If you choose to tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We don't see or store your payment details. The app only remembers on your phone that you've tipped, so it can say thanks.

Backups and sharing

If Back up to iCloud automatically is on and iCloud Drive is turned on, the app saves a backup to your own iCloud Drive each day you change something, and keeps the last 10 days plus one backup a month for a year. They go to your Apple account, not to us, and only you can see them. You can turn this off in the app's Settings.

If you save a backup, export your shifts or share a picture of your roster, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it (for example Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or Messages).

Email

If you email us for support or feedback, we'll only use your email to reply.

If you use Send feedback in the app, the email is filled in with your app version, iOS version, screen size, theme, platoon, leave group and any recent error messages, so we can help. You can read and change it before you send it, and nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Backups in iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them: open the Files app, go to iCloud Drive and delete the Firies Friend folder. Backup files you saved or shared yourself stay wherever you put them.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Not an official app

Firies Friend is not made, endorsed or supported by ACT Fire & Rescue, the ACT Emergency Services Agency, the ACT Government, the UFU or any other fire service.
